The opportunity

In Indian D2C, a sale isn’t final until the door opens.

Cash on delivery is how much of India still prefers to buy, and for furniture it raises the stakes. A customer who hasn’t heard from the brand by the time the truck arrives can simply say no.

The Sleep Company grew into India’s leading sleep solutions brand on its SmartGRID mattresses, chairs and ergonomic furniture, sold online and through experience centres in several cities. Every refused delivery cost two trips and a customer’s confidence. The calls meant to prevent it were made by hand, in office hours, often days after the order.

Its leaders wanted the first conversation after a purchase to feel like the brand: prompt, personal and in the customer’s own language. And they wanted it to hold on the busiest sale day of the year.

The decision

Don’t hire more callers. Change who makes the first call.

The obvious fix was a bigger calling team. It would have raised the cost of every order and still stopped at the end of the working day. The Sleep Company chose to put an AI agent on the first call instead, with clear limits: it confirms, schedules and records, and everything else goes to a person.

It chose devx because the team already knew the business from the inside. devx had rebuilt the storefront in 2024 and was connecting the website and the experience centres on retailOS, so the order data, the systems and the people were already familiar.
