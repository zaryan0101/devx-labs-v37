Context

A premium mattress, paid for at the door.

The Sleep Company is India’s leading sleep solutions brand, known for its SmartGRID technology across mattresses, chairs and ergonomic furniture. It sells nationwide, and a large share of its orders are paid cash on delivery.

For a high-value furniture order, the confirmation call is the first promise the brand keeps after the sale. When it comes late, customers start to doubt, some change their minds, and a refused delivery sends a truck out and back for nothing. The goal was to confirm every order on the day it was placed, at any volume, without growing the calling team.

The problem

Calls came days after the order, and only in office hours.

Confirmation was a last-mile problem dressed as a customer service one. A team of people called each COD order by hand, within business hours. On sale days the queue outgrew the team, and most customers waited days to hear from the brand.

Approach

Don’t add callers. Change who makes the first call.

The key decision was to put an agent on the first call, with clear limits: it confirms, schedules and records, and anything else goes to a person. That meant it had to sound like the customer, act inside the call, and know when to hand over.

{{i:wave}}

Hinglish voice, under 100 ms

A low-latency voice agent with voice activity detection, fluent in the mix of Hindi and English customers actually speak.

{{i:phone}}

200+ calls at once

Promise pooling runs hundreds of simultaneous calls, so a sale day never builds a queue.

{{i:book}}

A knowledge base in the brand’s voice

Retrieval over product, SmartGRID and warranty content keeps answers accurate and on tone.

{{i:check}}

Actions inside the call

Verifies the order ID, books the delivery slot, confirms payment and raises a ticket while the customer is still on the line.

{{i:db}}

Live sync with the systems of record

Every call updates Zendesk, Google Sheets and DynamoDB, so operations sees what the customer heard.

{{i:headset}}

Voicemail, and a person when needed

Detects voicemail, leaves a message and reschedules; transfers to a person in the same call when it should.
